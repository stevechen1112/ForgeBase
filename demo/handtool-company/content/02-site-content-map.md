## 4. Conversion Architecture

### High-intent conversion points

- Request a Quote buttons on every product and application page.
- Download technical sheet gate on selected products.
- Contact sales CTA on capabilities and OEM pages.
- Comparison pages linking to product selectors and RFQ.

### Mid-intent conversion points

- Ask about private label packaging.
- Request sample availability.
- Download OEM checklist.

### Low-intent engagement points

- FAQ browsing
- certification verification
- capability page exploration

## 5. Content Depth Standard

Each important page should include more than generic marketing copy.

Minimum expectations:
- every category page explains buyer use cases and typical sourcing concerns.
- every product page includes functional summary, specification table, application fit, and conversion path.
- every application page frames problem, requirement, and recommended tool families.
- every capability page explains operational benefit, not just internal process.
