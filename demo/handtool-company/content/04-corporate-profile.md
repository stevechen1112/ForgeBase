## Founder Background

Ethan K. Liao started NorthForge after more than a decade in Taiwan's precision metalworking and export tooling sector. Early in his career he worked closely with buyers who were frustrated by unstable quality between approved samples and mass production, unclear drawing control, and inconsistent export paperwork from smaller suppliers.

He founded NorthForge with a narrow initial focus: produce fastening and service-tool components with tighter process discipline and better customer-facing communication. As the business matured, the company expanded into finished hand tools, insulated tools, and custom toolkit programs for European and North American importers.

The founder's operating philosophy remains central to the brand: buyers do not only purchase tools; they purchase confidence that specifications, packaging, documents, and repeat orders will stay under control.

## Company Story

NorthForge grew in stages rather than through one dramatic expansion. The first stage was component-level manufacturing for socket and ratchet-related products. The second stage added finished-tool assembly and private-label packaging. The third stage expanded into insulated electrical tools and more compliance-sensitive export programs. The current stage focuses on becoming a stronger program partner for tool brands, industrial distributors, and OEM buyers who need both engineering reliability and commercial flexibility.

The company positions itself between low-cost volume factories and high-overhead enterprise manufacturers. It is large enough to support recurring export programs and custom development, but still responsive enough to work with mid-sized buyers that need direct communication and faster iteration.

## Corporate Milestones

- 2001: NorthForge established in Taichung as a forging and machining supplier for industrial hardware components.
- 2004: Began producing socket and ratchet-related components for export distributors.
- 2007: Added finished assembly lines for professional hand tool programs.
- 2011: Launched private-label packaging and custom kit assembly service for European importers.
- 2014: Expanded into insulated hand tools and electrical maintenance product programs.
- 2017: Built dedicated QC workflow for torque verification and traceability records.
- 2020: Upgraded export documentation and carton-labeling process for mixed-SKU programs.
- 2023: Completed packaging-center optimization for OEM toolkit assembly and retail-ready fulfillment.

## Quality and Documentation System

### Quality approach
NorthForge uses a layered quality process designed for export buyers who care about repeatability and post-shipment claim reduction.

### Typical controls
- incoming raw material verification
- dimensional inspection against approved drawings
- hardness and finish verification
- torque performance verification for torque-related tools
- random sampling before final packing
- carton and labeling inspection before shipment

## Public-Facing Summary

NorthForge Tools Co., Ltd. is a Taiwan-based OEM/ODM manufacturer of professional hand tools, insulated tools, torque tools, and industrial toolkit programs.
