# NorthForge Visual System

## Brand Direction

NorthForge should look like a serious export manufacturer, not a startup SaaS brand and not a cheap trading company catalog.

The visual language should communicate:
- mechanical precision
- industrial trust
- export readiness
- workshop practicality
- documentation discipline

## Color System

### Core colors
- Deep Navy: `#142235`
- Steel Blue: `#2F5D7C`
- Signal Orange: `#E46F2E`
- Alloy Gray: `#74808B`
- Mist Gray: `#E8ECEF`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

### Usage rules
- Deep Navy is the anchor color for headers, wordmark, and high-trust sections.
- Steel Blue supports technical and manufacturing language.
- Signal Orange is reserved for CTA emphasis and small brand-energy accents.
- Gray tones should make the interface feel industrial and structured, not fashionable.

## Typography Direction

### Recommended feel
- strong geometric sans for headlines
- clean readable grotesk or humanist sans for body copy
- no decorative or luxury styling

### Visual tone
- assertive headlines
- compressed spacing in hero areas
- structured subhead blocks
- table/spec-friendly body typography

## Photography Direction

### Preferred image style
- realistic industrial photography
- crisp metal texture and controlled lighting
- workshop, assembly, inspection, packaging, and export scenes
- product shots on matte neutral backgrounds
- occasional shallow depth of field for premium feel

### Avoid
- hyper-staged corporate handshake imagery
- overly glossy consumer-product marketing
- AI fantasy factory environments
- cinematic orange-teal effects
- exaggerated sparks, smoke, or heroic nonsense

## Product Image Language

Each major product line should use a consistent image pattern:
- front hero shot on neutral background
- secondary angle for mechanism or grip detail
- optional in-use contextual shot
- optional packaging shot for toolkit programs

## Factory / About Page Image Language

Factory images should show:
- organized workstations
- inspection activity
- assembly benches
- packaged finished goods
- export-readiness scenes

The goal is credibility through order and process, not scale theater.

## Certification Badge Direction

Badge visuals should feel documentation-driven and clean.
- white or pale neutral background
- navy text or dark gray text
- thin outlines
- restrained accent color
- no loud gradients or fake embossing

## Icon Direction

Icons should use:
- 1.5px to 2px strokes
- square or slightly rounded geometry
- technical, tool-oriented metaphor
- no playful cartoon shapes

## Website Section Visual Rules

### Homepage hero
- one strong factory-plus-product composition
- clean headline over dark overlay or low-noise background
- must support export-manufacturer trust within 2 seconds

### Category pages
- one category hero image each
- one or two supporting product detail crops

### Product pages
- product hero + one mechanism/detail visual + downloadable PDF mockup if available

### Capabilities pages
- process photography: packaging, inspection, assembly, labeling, documentation

### About page
- founder story should be supported by factory/process imagery, not portrait-centric branding

## Demo Priority Order

If only part of the asset system gets built first, prioritize in this order:
1. logo system
2. homepage hero
3. five category hero images
4. about page hero and one factory support image
5. top six featured product images
6. OEM line card / PDF cover visual
